# 42 WORKS STUDIO — PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE & USER GUIDE

---

## 1. WHAT IS 42 WORKS STUDIO?
**42 WORKS Studio** is an all-in-one AI creative platform that creates professional marketing posters, 14-second animated video reels, and ready-to-publish social media campaigns in just a few seconds.

You do not need any design skills, Photoshop knowledge, or video editing experience. You simply describe what occasion, campaign, or product you want to promote (for example: *"Chhath Puja celebration"*, *"Diwali lights"*, *"SaaS analytics software"*, or *"Artisanal coffee"*), and it automatically creates:
1. **Studio-Quality Visual Art**: A high-resolution picture created to match your exact prompt with clean headroom and no baked-in artifacts.
2. **14-Second Cinema Video Reels (9:16 Vertical)**: Realistic animated video reels powered by **Wan 2.2 Neural Diffusion** featuring genuine physical human acting (such as moving hands in a smooth clockwise circular aarti motion, gentle devotional smiles, flickering diya flames, and natural water ripples).
3. **Smart Typography & Colors**: Catchy headlines and meaningful quotes with high-contrast colors automatically chosen by Multimodal Vision AI to fit the background.
4. **Interactive Text Editor for Poster & Video**: Full drag-and-drop, stretch, scale, rotation, and color pickers working directly on both the poster canvas and the live video reel.
5. **Complete Social Media Posts**: Ready-to-publish captions with structured bullet points, emojis, and hashtags.
6. **One-Click Publishing & Scheduling**: Post immediately to **LinkedIn** and **Instagram**, or schedule posts for any future date and time.

## 4. HOW THE STUDIO WORKSPACE WORKS

The **Studio** page is where creation starts:

### A. Upcoming Occasions (Auto-Calendar)
- Positioned right below the top heading.
- Displays the **next 5 upcoming festivals and holidays** (such as Chhath Puja, Bhai Dooj, Thanksgiving, Diwali, and New Year).
- **How to use it**: Click any holiday card, and the app will instantly populate the prompt box with an authentic creative brief.

### B. Prompt Input Box
- Type your topic, product, or campaign description in plain words.
- **Smart Predictive Autocomplete**: As you type, the studio suggests natural continuations in subtle grey text. Press the **Tab** key or **Right Arrow** to accept the suggestion.

### C. Aspect Ratio Selection
Select the format that fits your primary distribution channel:
- **Square (1:1)**: Standard feed posts for LinkedIn, Instagram, and Facebook.
- **Portrait (4:5)**: Tall mobile feed format that maximizes vertical engagement.
- **Landscape (16:9)**: Horizontal layout for website hero banners, blogs, and Twitter/X.
- **Story / Reel (9:16)**: Full-screen vertical format for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Stories.

### D. Generation Actions
- **Generate Cinematic Poster**: Produces the visual art, headline, story quote, and caption in 10–12 seconds.
- **Convert to Video Reel**: Synthesizes a 14-second animated video reel with character action, ambient motion, and a professional brand outro card.

## 5. REVIEW, TYPOGRAPHY EDITING & RE-COMPOSITION

Once created, the interactive review window opens, featuring your asset on the left and full editing controls on the right.

### A. AI-Driven Default Placement
- The Multimodal Vision AI scans the clean background image to find open safe space (like the open sky above a person's head) and evaluates background lighting.
- It chooses optimal high-contrast colors (like radiant gold or crisp white on dusk/dawn backgrounds) so text is readable without covering faces, hands, or offerings.

### B. Interactive Canvas Editing (Poster & Video Reel)
- **Unified Draggable Box**: An interactive bounding box surrounds the headline and reflection quote. Click and drag to position text anywhere on the canvas.
- **Stretch Handles**: Drag the East or West side handles to adjust text box width horizontally.
- **Proportional Scaling**: Pull any of the four corner handles to scale text size smoothly.
- **Free Rotation Knob**: Grab the top circular handle to rotate text, or click **0° Reset** to snap back to level.
- **Quick Position Presets**:
  - **Top (16%)**: Places text in the safe upper headroom.
  - **Upper (26%)**: Places text across the upper third.
  - **Bottom (80%)**: Places text in the lower foreground.

### C. Live Video Typography Editing & Approval
- When viewing the **Video Reel** tab, clicking **"Reposition Text"** opens an interactive typography preview directly over the video player.
- You can drag, resize, rotate, or re-color the text right on the video.
- A dedicated Action Bar appears below the video:
  $$\text{[Video Text Position: } Y\% \text{]} \quad\longrightarrow\quad \text{[Cancel]} \quad \text{[✓ Approve & Place Here]}$$
- Clicking **"Approve & Place Here"** updates the video reel in **under 6 seconds** using a high-performance streaming parallel pipeline, locking the text in your exact approved position.

### D. Typography & Color Palette Controls (Right Sidebar)
- **Headline Textarea**: Edit the primary title words.
- **Story Quote Textarea**: Edit the philosophical reflection quote.
- **Color Presets**: Toggle between *Light on Dark* (White & Gold) or *Dark on Light* (Dark Slate & Bronze).
- **Custom Color Pickers**: Select custom hex colors for the Headline, Quote, and Diamond Accent Divider.
- **Apply Instant Revision Button**: Saves your custom text and re-composites both the poster and the video reel in seconds.

### E. Social Caption & Hashtag Manager
- **Structured Caption**: Generates an opening narrative hook, structured bullet-point insights with tasteful emojis, an inspiring closing thought, and an engaging question to prompt audience discussion.
- **Copy Caption**: Copies the entire post text and hashtags with a single click.
- **Hashtag Editor**: Add or refine hashtags matching your brand or festival.

## 8. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

**Q: Do I need graphic design or video editing software?**
A: No. The application handles photography synthesis, character motion choreography, typography design, and video encoding automatically in the browser.

**Q: How does the AI know where to place the text?**
A: A Multimodal Vision model scans the visual elements (people, faces, hands, held props, horizons) and automatically centers the text in clean negative space (such as open sky) so important subjects are never obstructed.

**Q: Can I put the text in a different spot if I want to?**
A: Yes! On both the poster canvas and the video reel, click and drag the text to any position you choose, then click "Approve & Place Here" or "Apply Instant Revision" to lock it in that exact location.

**Q: How long does it take to update the video when I move the text?**
A: Thanks to our streaming parallel FFmpeg pipe engine, re-rendering the typography onto the video reel takes approximately 6 seconds.

**Q: Can I schedule a post for next week?**
A: Yes. Under Multi-Channel Dispatch, choose your platform, select the schedule clock icon, set your desired date and time, and confirm. The automated scheduler handles the publishing for you.
